The Premise

Success is a skill. So is intentional dating.

You learned to build a career, a body, a portfolio, a life. No one ever taught you to build a relationship with the same rigor — so you've been improvising in the one place that matters most. Intentional dating is the deliberate practice of knowing exactly who you are, exactly what you want, and exactly how to recognize it when it's standing in front of you.

I don't do scripts. I don't do matchmaking. I don't do therapy. I help you see the pattern that's quietly costing you — and become the person the right partner will recognize.

Who It's For

For the accomplished,
not the average.

You're successful, self-aware, and used to getting results. Yet dating has stayed the one area where effort hasn't translated. That isn't a flaw — it's a blind spot you can't see from inside it.

This is for you if…

  • You've achieved a great deal professionally, but your personal life hasn't kept pace.
  • You're tired of the apps, the noise, and conflicting advice from everyone around you.
  • You want to filter for the right people — fast — whether you stay on the apps or leave them.
  • You value discretion and want guidance, not a database of strangers.
  • You're ready to do the honest work, not just collect tips.

This is not for you if…

  • You want someone to find and introduce matches for you — that's matchmaking, and I'm happy to refer you.
  • You're looking for pickup tactics or quick hacks.
  • You're not willing to look honestly at your own patterns.
  • You want validation, not change — endless advice, no action, and the problem is always someone else.
The Framework

The 4-P Method

Every client moves through four pieces. Some need a year on one of them. Some get through three in a single session. The order never changes — because each one is built on the last.

P

Presence

Your inner state before the date. Self-confidence, nervous-system regulation, the version of you that walks into the room.

P

Picture

Clarity on who you actually want — and what you actually need. Not what your parents wanted. Not what looks good on paper.

P

Presentation

How you show up. Your profile, your photos, your conversation, your first-date energy — aligned with who you really are.

P

Progression

How a connection becomes a relationship. When to escalate, when to step back, when to walk away while you still want to stay.

It's the inner work that the rest of dating is downstream of.
